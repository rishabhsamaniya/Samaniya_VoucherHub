Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Samaniya VoucherHub. Can't find an answer? Contact us.

General
What is Samaniya VoucherHub?
+
Samaniya VoucherHub is India's leading platform for buying discounted digital vouchers and gift cards. We partner directly with top brands like Amazon, Flipkart, Netflix, Steam, and hundreds more to offer you genuine vouchers at up to 40% discount. Once you purchase, the voucher code is delivered instantly to your email and displayed on screen.
Are the vouchers genuine and valid?
+
Absolutely. Every voucher on Samaniya VoucherHub is sourced directly from official brand partners. We guarantee 100% genuine codes. In the rare event a code doesn't work, we offer immediate replacement or a full refund — no questions asked.
Is there a minimum order value?
+
No minimum order value! You can purchase even a single ₹100 voucher. There are no hidden charges — the price you see is the price you pay. Processing fees are also completely free.
Payment
What payment methods are accepted?
+
We accept all major payment methods: UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay), Net Banking (all major Indian banks), and digital wallets (Paytm, Amazon Pay, Mobikwik, Freecharge). All transactions are secured with 256-bit SSL encryption.
Is my payment information secure?
+
Yes. Samaniya VoucherHub uses bank-grade 256-bit SSL encryption for all transactions. We are PCI-DSS compliant and never store your card or banking details on our servers. Payments are processed through certified payment gateways, ensuring your financial information is always protected.
Can I use coupons or promo codes?
+
Yes! Samaniya VoucherHub regularly offers coupon codes for additional savings. You can enter a coupon code during checkout. Try codes like SAVE10 (10% off), VH20 (20% off), or NEWUSER (15% off for first-time buyers). Subscribe to our newsletter to receive exclusive codes in your inbox.
Delivery
How quickly do I receive my voucher?
+
Voucher codes are delivered instantly — typically within 5–10 seconds of successful payment. The code is displayed on your screen immediately after purchase and also sent to your registered email address. No waiting, no delays.
What if I don't receive my voucher code?
+
First, check your spam/junk folder. If you still don't receive it within 5 minutes, log into your Samaniya VoucherHub account and go to "My Orders" — all codes are saved there. If you still face issues, contact our 24/7 support via live chat or email at support@samaniyavoucherhub.in and we'll resolve it immediately.
Refunds
What is your refund policy?
+
We offer a 100% refund guarantee. If a voucher code doesn't work, we'll replace it immediately. If we can't provide a replacement, we'll issue a full refund to your original payment method within 3–5 business days. Refunds for unused, unshared codes can also be requested within 7 days of purchase.
Can I exchange a voucher for another brand?
+
Voucher exchanges are possible within 24 hours of purchase, provided the code has not been used or shared. Please contact our support team with your order ID and the reason for exchange. Exchanges are subject to availability and are processed as store credit on your Samaniya VoucherHub account.
